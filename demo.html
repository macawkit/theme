02

Visual language

surfaces · markers · layers
Panel .panel A region of the page and the outermost surface. Everything else lives inside one.
Well .well Sunk into a surface. Holds a group of data or controls so they read as one.
Card .card A self-contained unit inside a panel, made to be repeated.
Box .box · .active The smallest frame: one glyph or one value. Lights up when active.
