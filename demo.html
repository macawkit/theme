Pages, nav.pages: a link for each, aria-current="page" on the one shown, rel="prev" and rel="next" on the way back and on, in a list or straight in it. What is not a link is the gap of the pages left out. .small is the compact one. On the first page the way back has no href and is aria-disabled, and so is the way on from the last. The queue below has a small one under its table, its way back and on only icons with a title, square.

Bounces are kept for five days and then returned, see the queue manual or ask the postmaster. A link already visited stays the same.
